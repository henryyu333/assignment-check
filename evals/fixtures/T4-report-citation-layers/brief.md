# MGT3101 Organisational Behaviour — Report 2

**Topic:** Does remote work improve productivity?
**Due:** Week 12, Friday 23:59
**Length:** 750–850 words (excluding the reference list)

## Task

Write a report that answers the question in the title. Your report must:

1. take a clear position on whether remote work improves productivity, and support it with evidence;
2. cite **at least three sources**, of which **at least two must be peer-reviewed** (journal articles or peer-reviewed conference papers). Blogs, consultancy publications, news websites and lecture slides do not count towards the two peer-reviewed sources;
3. format all in-text citations and the reference list in **APA 7th edition**;
4. discuss the limitations of the evidence you rely on.

## Marking notes

- The marker checks the reference list against the claims it is attached to: a source that exists but does not support the sentence it is cited for is treated as a citation problem, not as a supported claim.
- Every major claim in the report should be traceable to a source in the reference list.
- Submit as .docx or .md. A reference list is required; no cover page.
