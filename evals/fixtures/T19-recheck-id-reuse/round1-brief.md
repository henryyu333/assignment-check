# Media & Society 201 — Critical Report Brief

**Deadline:** Friday 26 September, 23:59
**Format:** one .pdf or .docx file, 1.5 line spacing

**Statement to write about:**

> "Social media platforms have done more harm than good to public discourse."

**Task.** Write a critical report about the statement above. You may agree or disagree with it, but the report must argue a position rather than merely summarise the debate.

**Requirements.**

1. **Length.** The report must be 600 words (±10%), excluding the title, the section headings and the reference list.
2. **Structure.** Include a title; an introduction that states your position in one or two sentences; a body with clearly labelled sections; and a conclusion that restates what you have argued.
3. **Position.** Take a clear position on the statement and support it with reasoning of your own.
4. **Counter-argument.** Discuss at least one counter-argument to your position and respond to it.
5. **Sources.** Use at least three credible sources (academic, governmental or quality journalism) and cite them in the text.
6. **Reference list.** Add a reference list at the end in APA 7th style.

**Marking.** The report is marked on the completeness of the six requirements above, the clarity of the position taken, and the quality of the reasoning. There is no separate weighting for individual requirements.
