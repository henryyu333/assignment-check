# Marking Rubric — BUS 3420 Individual Case Study

Total: 100 marks, awarded against four criteria. Each criterion's weight is given in its heading.

**Sub-items listed inside a criterion are components of that criterion. They are not separately weighted and must not be marked as independent criteria.**

## Criterion A — Problem framing (20%)

The report states the decision problem clearly **and** explains why that decision matters.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | The decision facing the Board is stated precisely, and its significance is explained concretely (for example, with reference to NFG's service, cost or risk position). It is clear what the report will and will not decide. |
| 65–79 | The decision is stated clearly and its significance is explained, but the explanation is general or only partly tied to the case. |
| 50–64 | The decision is stated, but its significance is asserted rather than explained. |
| 0–49 | The decision problem is unclear, or the report moves straight into description with no framing. |

## Criterion B — Analysis (40%)

The report builds a clear and reasoned argument (**B1**), considers at least one counter-perspective that challenges its own recommendation (**B2**), and supports its conclusion with evidence drawn from the analysis (**B3**).

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | The argument is coherent and directed; a genuine counter-perspective is stated and answered; the conclusion follows from evidence presented in the report. |
| 65–79 | The argument is clear and evidence-based, but the counter-perspective is weak, partial or absent, or the conclusion is only partly supported by the evidence given. |
| 50–64 | The argument is largely descriptive; evidence is listed rather than weighed; there is no serious engagement with an opposing view. |
| 0–49 | Little or no argument; assertions are not supported. |

## Criterion C — Use of evidence (25%)

The report uses at least four credible sources (**C1**) and presents at least one figure or table whose content is consistent with the text (**C2**).

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | Four or more credible sources, used where they matter; the figure or table is accurate, labelled and consistent with the text. |
| 65–79 | Four or more sources but unevenly used, or a figure or table that is present but adds little or is not fully consistent with the text. |
| 50–64 | Fewer than four credible sources, or a figure or table that is inconsistent with the text. |
| 0–49 | No usable sources and no evidence display. |

## Criterion D — Presentation (15%)

Structure, Harvard citation format, and word count.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | Required sections present, with the required headings, in the required order; Harvard in-text citation and reference list correct; within the word limit. |
| 65–79 | Sections present; minor citation errors; within, or only negligibly outside, the word limit. |
| 50–64 | A required section is missing or misordered; citation format inconsistent; outside the word limit. |
| 0–49 | Structure does not follow the brief. |
