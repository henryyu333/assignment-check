# BI 210 — Assignment 2: Data Story Report

**Course**: BI 210 Data Storytelling
**Format**: individual written report
**Length**: 500–700 words
**Data**: `retention_quarterly.csv` (provided in the module resources): one row per signup cohort, quarterly retention from 2021-Q1 to 2024-Q2, for a subscription language-learning product.

## Task

Write a short data story on the retention trend of the product, using the provided cohort dataset.

## Requirements

1. Submit a written report of **500–700 words**.
2. Use clear **section headings** (context, method, findings, limitations).
3. Include **at least one chart or figure** in the report.
4. Every figure must be **consistent with the numbers and claims in the text**: the same quantities, the same direction of change, the same period labels.
5. Every figure must be **understandable on its own**: it needs a title, labelled axes, and units.
6. Cite **at least two sources** for the retention benchmarks you compare the product against (for example SaaS or consumer-subscription retention benchmarks).
7. State the **main limitation** of your analysis.

## Marking

Marks are awarded for the clarity of the story, the correctness of the analysis, and the quality of the figures.
