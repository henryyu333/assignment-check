# CS 402 Assignment 3 — Convergence Behaviour of Optimisation Methods

**Course**: CS 402 Numerical Optimisation
**Format**: Individual written submission (single document)
**Maximum length**: 700 words

## Task

Compare gradient descent and simulated annealing on **convergence behaviour**, and state which one is preferable for a large sparse problem.

## Requirements

Your submission must:

1. Explain the convergence behaviour of gradient descent: the update rule, the role of the step size / learning rate, the conditions under which it converges, and its practical limitations.
2. Explain the convergence behaviour of simulated annealing: the acceptance rule, the role of the temperature schedule, the conditions under which it converges, and its practical limitations.
3. **Compare the two methods on the same dimensions** (for example convergence rate, convergence guarantees, sensitivity to hyperparameters, and behaviour on large or non-convex problems), and make a comparative judgement about their convergence behaviour. Describing each method separately is not a comparison.
4. State which method is preferable for a large sparse problem and justify the choice.
5. Keep the whole submission to a maximum of 700 words.
6. Use section headings.

Marks are awarded on convergence explanation, comparative analysis, and the justification of the recommendation.
