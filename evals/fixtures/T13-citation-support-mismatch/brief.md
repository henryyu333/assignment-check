# POL-204 · Education Policy Brief — Assignment Brief

## Task

Write a policy brief that argues for **one** evidence-based early literacy intervention for primary-school students (ages 5–11). The brief is written for a national education ministry that is deciding where to spend a limited literacy budget next year.

## Requirements

1. **Length.** 550–650 words, excluding the title and the reference list.
2. **Topic.** The brief must be about an early literacy intervention for primary-school students, not about secondary or higher education.
3. **Evidence for every major claim.** Every major claim in the body of the brief must be supported by a citation to a scholarly source. A statement that goes beyond what the cited work actually shows does not count as supported.
4. **Sources.** Cite at least four distinct sources, and include a reference list at the end of the brief.
5. **Referencing style.** Use APA 7th edition for both in-text citations and the reference list.
6. **Policy Recommendations section.** Include a section headed "Policy Recommendations" containing two or three recommendations. Each recommendation must be traceable to evidence presented earlier in the brief.
7. **Definition.** Define the term "early literacy intervention" in the introduction, before the term is used in the argument.

## Marking

There is no rubric for this task. The marker will read the brief against the requirements above, with particular attention to requirement 3.
