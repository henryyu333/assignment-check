# PSY2004 Cognitive Psychology — Assignment 2 (Individual Essay)

**Due:** Friday 9 October, 23:59 (Turnitin, School of Psychology)
**Submit as:** .docx or .md

## Task

Write a short essay on how psychologists explain judgment under uncertainty, using **dual-process theory** as your starting point.

Your essay must address all four of the numbered requirements below. Your tutor checks each one.

1. Define **dual-process theory in your own words** — do not copy the lecture slides or a dictionary definition — and give **one everyday example** that illustrates the difference between the two types of processing.
2. Explain the disagreement between the **Kahneman** tradition and the **Gigerenzer** tradition about **whether heuristics are useful**. State what each side claims.
3. On the dimension of **predictive accuracy** — how well each tradition's account predicts people's actual judgments and decisions — **judge which of the two traditions you find more persuasive**, and justify your judgment.
4. Cite at least **two peer-reviewed sources** and list them in a reference list formatted in **APA 7th edition**. Lecture slides, textbooks, blogs and news websites do not count towards the two peer-reviewed sources.

## Format

- **600–750 words** in the body of the essay, excluding the reference list.
- Continuous prose. Brief headings are permitted.
- No cover page required.
- Markers do not require an abstract or a title page.
