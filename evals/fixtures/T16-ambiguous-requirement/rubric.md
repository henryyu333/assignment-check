# IR-310 · Marking Rubric (Essay 2)

Each criterion is assessed against the descriptors below. The module attaches no marks or weights to individual criteria; the marker uses the descriptors to place the essay.

| Criterion | Excellent | Good | Satisfactory | Weak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Argument and position | States a clear position and defends it consistently, engaging with the strongest objection | States a position and defends it, with some engagement with objections | States a position but defends it unevenly | Position unclear or simply asserted |
| Use of sources | Wide range of high-quality sources, integrated into the argument | Several high-quality sources, used accurately | Sources present but limited or mostly descriptive | Few, unreliable, or misused sources |
| Structure and clarity | Introduction, body and conclusion clearly organised and signposted | Clear organisation with a few rough edges | Organisation present but uneven | Structure hard to follow |
| Definition of the key concept | Key concept defined precisely and used consistently | Key concept defined and mostly used consistently | Key concept mentioned but loosely defined | Key concept left undefined |
