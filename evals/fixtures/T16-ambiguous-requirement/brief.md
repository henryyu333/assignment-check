# IR-310 · Essay 2 — Digital Sovereignty and the Cloud

## Context

This is the second essay in the module. The first essay examined the technical layers of the internet; this one turns to the political question of who can govern data once it has left the jurisdiction in which it was created. Seminars in weeks 5–8 covered the GDPR, the Schrems litigation and the debate over "cyber sovereignty" in international relations.

## Question

"To what extent can states exercise sovereignty over data stored in the cloud?" Answer this question in an essay of **700 words**.

## Requirements

1. **Length.** 700 words, ±10% (so between 630 and 770 words), excluding the title and the reference list.
2. **Structure.** An introduction that states your position on the question, two body sections that develop the argument, and a conclusion that answers the question.
3. **Definition.** Define the term "digital sovereignty" explicitly in the introduction, before you use it in the argument.
4. **Sources.** Support your argument with a wide range of high-quality sources.
5. **Reference list.** Include a reference list in APA 7th edition.
6. **Concrete example.** Include at least one concrete example (a law, a court case, or an incident) and explain how it illustrates your argument.
