# Campus Transport Memo

## Decision

The university needs a transport option that can reduce short car trips between the main teaching buildings and the residence halls.

## Option A — Electric shuttle buses

Electric shuttles can carry more passengers per trip and can serve students who cannot cycle. They require vehicles, charging access and a scheduled route.

## Option B — Expanded bike-share

Bike-share can be distributed across several docking points and does not require a fixed timetable. It depends more heavily on weather and on users being able and willing to cycle.

## Comparison

Bike-share would require less upfront spending than purchasing a shuttle fleet.

## Recommendation

I recommend expanded bike-share because the lower upfront cost makes it easier to launch without a large capital purchase. The university could then review usage before deciding whether a shuttle route is also needed.
