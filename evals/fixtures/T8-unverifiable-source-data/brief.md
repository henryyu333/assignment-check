# BUS 2140 Business Analytics — Report 1: Student Experience Survey

**Length:** 600 words (±10%), excluding the reference list, tables and figures
**Submission:** one file (PDF or Markdown) uploaded to the module site
**Data:** `survey.csv`, published on the module site. **Do not submit the dataset or your analysis code** — your tutor already has the data.
**Deadline:** Friday 6 March 2026, 23:59

## Task

The faculty has asked for a short analytics report on the 2026 Student Experience Survey. Using the dataset supplied on the module site, answer the research question:

> How satisfied are students with their programme, and how does satisfaction vary across year groups?

The dataset contains one row per respondent. The variables you will need are `satisfaction` (1–5), `year` (1–4), `continued` (whether the respondent progressed into the next year), `support_rating` (1–5) and `response_id`.

## Required content

Your report must:

1. state the research question and explain why it matters to the faculty;
2. calculate and report **three metrics** from the dataset:
   - the mean overall satisfaction (1–5 scale);
   - the first-year retention rate (as a percentage);
   - the share of respondents who rated academic support 4 or 5 (as a percentage);
3. state, for each metric, how it was calculated — numerator, denominator, and any records excluded;
4. present **Figure 1**: a chart of mean satisfaction by year group;
5. describe the method in no more than one paragraph;
6. include a limitations section covering at least two limitations of the survey;
7. cite at least two sources in Harvard style;
8. stay within the word limit.

## Required structure

Use these five sections, in this order:

1. Introduction and research question
2. Method
3. Results
4. Limitations
5. Conclusion

Use only the dataset provided. Do not substitute external or simulated data for the survey figures.
