# COM 220 — Short Essay 2: Justifying a Visualisation Choice

**Course**: COM 220 Data Communication
**Weight**: 10% of the module mark
**Length**: maximum 600 words (excluding the reference list)
**Format**: individual written submission

## Task

Choose one data visualisation that you have actually used (in coursework, an internship, or a personal project) and analyse that choice.

## Requirements

Your submission must do all four of the following:

1. **Identify the visualisation** you used and **describe the dataset and the context** in which it was used (what the data was about, who the audience was, and what the chart was supposed to communicate).
2. Explain **at least two advantages** of that visualisation choice **for that context**.
3. Explain **at least two disadvantages or limitations** of that visualisation choice.
4. Propose **one specific improvement** to the visualisation, and explain **which of the disadvantages in (3) it addresses** and how.

## Marking

Marks are awarded for the clarity of the context, the accuracy of the advantages and limitations you identify, and the quality of the proposed improvement.
